# Astra Destiny — Topical Authority Keyword Strategy

Last updated: 2026-11-16 | Strategy: Low-KD long-tail informational content + tool pages to build domain authority before competing for head terms.

---

## Strategy Rationale

Competing for "horoscope" (3.35M) or "birth chart" (673K) requires high DR. Instead, we build **topical authority** through hundreds of low-competition, high-specificity pages that:

1. **Planet-in-house articles** (9 planets × 12 houses = 108 pages)
2. **Nakshatra profiles** (27 nakshatras × characteristics/compatibility = 54+ pages)
3. **Jaimini concept pages** (Atmakaraka, Darakaraka, Upapada, etc.)
4. **Tool landing pages** with dedicated SEO
5. **Western astrology calculators** (Chiron, Lilith, North Node — adjacent high-volume audience)
6. **Numerology calculators** (cross-selling to adjacent audience)

**Target filters (Ahrefs-equivalent):** KD 0–10, Volume 100–5,000, Word count 3+, Top-10 avg DR below 30.
