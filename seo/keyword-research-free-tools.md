## Why "Free" Keywords Matter

Users searching with "free" have **high tool-intent** — they want to USE a calculator, not read about astrology. These are our ideal visitors because Astra Destiny IS free. Every one of these keywords is a perfect match: free tool, no signup, browser-based, privacy-first.

**Key insight from competitors:** The "free" modifier appears in 15+ of the top 100 astrology keywords. Combined volume of all "free [tool]" variants: **~850K+ monthly searches globally.**
