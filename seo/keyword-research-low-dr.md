## ⚠️ Important Notes

1. **DR ≠ KD:** A keyword with low KD (few backlinks on ranking pages) can still have high-DR sites ranking — and vice versa. We checked both: the keywords above have confirmed low-DR competitors AND estimated low KD.

2. **Ahrefs DR4 launches Dec 1, 2026:** Ahrefs is rolling out a new DR model that scores subdomains separately. This will cause DR shifts across the industry. Monitor competitor DRs after this date.

3. **GrahAI is the key threat:** DR 13, growing +288%/mo, same tool-first model. They went from 0 to 167K/mo in 7 months. If we don't move fast on Jaimini + numerology keywords, they will consolidate.

4. **"Calculator" in title matters:** Nearly every low-DR success story has "calculator" prominently in their page title. This signals tool intent to Google and matches user search patterns.

5. **Client-side computation is table stakes:** All new competitors compute in the browser (Swiss Ephemeris / Astronomy Engine). We already do this — it's not a differentiator but a requirement.
